# 1. Current Position

GEORGE has moved beyond concept and core development.

The project is now in the transition:

**Refinement → Real-World Validation → Production → Portability**

The public and fundraising position must be precise:

> GEORGE has moved beyond concept and core development. We are refining, testing, and preparing the system for production and portability.

Do not claim that GEORGE is fully production-complete or enterprise-grade until the implementation, infrastructure, security, qualification, and operational audit verifies that status.

---

# 2. Early Access Is Not Just a Waitlist

Early Access has four simultaneous purposes:

1. Generate operating capital for continued development and production preparation.
2. Bring GEORGE into real-world use.
3. Identify the use cases where GEORGE creates the greatest value.
4. Refine and perfect GEORGE through actual usage patterns and explicit user feedback.

Early users are customers, not unpaid QA personnel.

Their benefit is early access, participation in GEORGE's development direction, potential founding benefits, and an opportunity to help determine where GEORGE becomes exceptional.

Core message:

> We're not raising money to start building GEORGE. We're inviting people into the process of refining, validating, expanding, and preparing GEORGE for production.

---

# 3. Privacy-Safe Use-Case Success Measurement

GEORGE must be able to learn where it is effective without turning private user conversations into a shared data pool.

Public-facing principle:

> **GEORGE learns where it can become more effective across real-world use cases without turning private conversations into a shared data pool.**

This principle should be shown to Early Access users and Founding Creators as a product benefit and trust boundary.

The objective is to measure patterns of effectiveness across categories using only the minimum metadata required to improve GEORGE.

Potential measurement dimensions, subject to implementation and privacy review:

- use-case category;
- selected GEORGE capability or support surface;
- delivery mode, such as Line or Cue;
- repeated use;
- session continuation or abandonment;
- latency/performance bands;
- provider/model class when necessary for performance and cost analysis;
- explicit usefulness signals;
- explicit progress-toward-objective signals;
- aggregate retention and repeat-use patterns.

The measurement objective is:

> **Measure what improves GEORGE, not who the user is.**

Do not treat user-specific continuity, private context, or unnecessary personal information as a general shared learning pool.

---

# 4. Use-Case Communities

GEORGE should be marketed to individual users while allowing users and creators to identify the situations where they expect to benefit most.

Initial candidate communities include:

- Sales
- Negotiation
- Founders / Entrepreneurship
- Two-way Content Creators
- Career / Interviews
- Leadership
- Communication Support
- Accessibility / Disability Outreach
- General / Other

These are entry points into GEORGE, not necessarily separate products.

The product question should remain outcome-oriented:

> **What are you trying to accomplish?**

Real-world usage should help determine which categories deserve deeper product refinement, dedicated experiences, marketing, and eventually portable or branded deployments.

---

# 5. Founding Creators

Founding Creators are not merely affiliate marketers.

They are distribution and real-world validation partners who introduce GEORGE to audiences that can reveal where the product creates exceptional value.

Creator flow:

**Accept → Get GEORGE access → Get personal link → Get QR → Get teleprompter-ready script → Use → Record → Publish → Earn → Help reveal where GEORGE matters most**

Each creator should have persistent attribution for users introduced through that creator, preserving the ability to implement sustainable first-payment or recurring qualifying revenue participation after unit economics are complete.

Do not finalize commission percentages before the financial audit.

The system should preserve, where appropriate:

- creator identifier;
- attributed user identifier or privacy-safe equivalent;
- attribution date;
- campaign/source;
- subscription tier;
- qualifying payment events;
- refund/chargeback status;
- commission status.

The exact schema and retention rules must be implemented consistently with the privacy doctrine.

---

# 6. Creator Value Proposition

Creators should understand the capability through what it allows them to do, not through explanations of GEORGE's internal process.

Core execution language:

> **GEORGE gives creators a different operational capability.**

> **You can open conversations you might otherwise avoid. Unfamiliar subjects. Unexpected questions. Negotiations. Interviews. Business discussions. Difficult conversations. You-name-it.**

> **A creator who might normally think, “I don't know enough about this to have that conversation,” can say: “Let's talk about it.”**

GEORGE's value should be expressed through action and possibility, without publicly explaining proprietary orchestration, provider routing, prompts, pipelines, or internal decision logic.

---

# 7. Mission

GEORGE is being built to make opportunity more equitable.

Opportunity should not belong only to those with wealth, status, pedigree, or privileged access.

**Ambition matters. Preparation matters. Intelligent communication could matter most.**

Intelligent communication can bring goals closer to fruition.

A useful execution expression is:

> **Your ambition sets the direction. Preparation builds the path. Intelligent communication brings your goals closer to fruition.**

GEORGE supports the user's purpose and agency. It should not be framed as replacing the user's judgment or guaranteeing outcomes.

---

# 8. Funding Thesis

The immediate question is not how much capital is required to invent GEORGE from scratch.

The audit must determine the minimum capital required to:

1. complete remaining refinement;
2. support real-world validation;
3. secure the necessary AI/provider resources;
4. maintain hosting and infrastructure;
5. harden the system for production;
6. support the first meaningful user and creator cohorts;
7. maintain an appropriate operating reserve.

The audit should produce three numbers:

1. **Minimum cash to get GEORGE operational for Early Access**
2. **Cash required for a credible enterprise-grade MVP**
3. **Recommended reserve for the first real user/creator cohort**

The initial requirement may be materially smaller than a traditional seed round. This must be verified from actual provider, hosting, infrastructure, and remaining-work requirements.

Do not reduce the campaign message to “we need money for OpenAI credits.” AI resources are one component of the production transition.

---

# 9. Portability and Future Creator Economics

GEORGE is being prepared for portability.

Future qualified creators may have the opportunity to build a branded relationship, deployment, or version around GEORGE's operational capability, subject to the eventual portability architecture, commercial model, and qualification requirements.

Do not prematurely promise white-label rights, revenue percentages, licensing terms, or branded deployments before the technical and financial audit is complete.

Potential economic layers:

1. Referral economics
2. Community economics
3. Qualified portable/branded deployment economics

Persistent attribution should preserve optionality without creating unsustainable promises.

---

# 10. Information Disclosure Doctrine

Public users and creators should understand what GEORGE can do without receiving the blueprint for how it does it.

Appropriate public concepts may include:

- Signals
- Complex Reasoning
- Memory
- Boardroom
- Fast Response
- Line
- Cue

The public product experience should describe outcomes and capabilities, not proprietary internals such as:

- transcript-processing implementation;
- signal-detection implementation;
- reasoning-stage mechanics;
- provider-routing rules;
- exact model-selection logic;
- prompt architectures;
- orchestration internals;
- latency-handling algorithms;
- private memory retrieval mechanics.

The principle is:

> **Show the capability. Protect the choreography.**
