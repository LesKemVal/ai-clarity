# GEORGE Early Access Fundraising Apparatus

**Status:** Working launch specification — not architectural authority  
**Branch:** `live-hub-runtime`  
**Created:** 2026-08-28

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the first operating apparatus for funding, communicating, testing, and validating GEORGE through Early Access.

It complements `docs/george/EARLY_ACCESS_FOUNDING_CREATOR_STRATEGY.md` and does not override production, runtime, operational, security, or architectural authorities.

The immediate objective is **not** to raise a traditional seed round and is **not** to scale immediately to 20,000 users.

The immediate objective is to raise enough operating capital to:

1. complete remaining refinement;
2. fund meaningful real-world testing;
3. secure required AI/provider resources;
4. maintain required hosting and infrastructure;
5. harden the product toward production readiness;
6. demonstrate GEORGE's potential with real users;
7. establish evidence for the next creator and user expansion wave.

Early Access funding and participation are therefore part of the product-validation loop.

---

# 2. Core Position

> **GEORGE has moved beyond concept and core development. We are refining, testing, and preparing the system for production and portability.**

The campaign must not imply that GEORGE is merely an idea awaiting construction.

It must also not claim that GEORGE is already fully production-complete or enterprise-grade until the relevant implementation, security, infrastructure, qualification, and operational audits establish that status.

The simple public proposition is:

> **Help us bring GEORGE through Early Access.**

Early users provide funding through their purchases or contributions and provide valuable real-world validation through actual use.

---

# 3. Mission

Primary mission language:

> **We believe a lack of substantial resources, social status, or pedigree should not exclude anyone from achieving their goals.**
>
> **We believe intelligent communication can make opportunity more equitable.**
>
> **We're building GEORGE so more people can prepare, communicate, and execute with intelligence in high-impact situations.**

Product framing:

> **Your ambition sets the direction. Preparation builds the path. Intelligent communication brings your goals closer to fruition.**

GEORGE should be framed around planning and execution, not around describing internal AI processes.

---

# 4. First-Wave Audience

The first wave is intentionally broader than a single professional vertical but concentrated around a common condition:

**People trying to accomplish something who may not have substantial resources, privileged access, expert preparation, or an established network.**

Priority audiences include:

- people seeking employment or advancement;
- aspiring founders and entrepreneurs;
- existing small-business owners;
- salespeople and telemarketers;
- independent professionals;
- people preparing for interviews;
- people preparing for negotiations;
- creators who regularly conduct two-way conversations;
- advocates and community leaders;
- accessibility and disability communities;
- educational and workforce communities;
- people navigating unfamiliar institutions or professional environments;
- people who want help organizing their thinking before acting;
- people who need communication support.

Political, advocacy, civic, demographic, professional, and other community creators may be appropriate where they see a legitimate potential benefit for themselves or the communities they serve.

GEORGE is **not** a political product. Creator alignment is based on audience needs and potential usefulness, not political agreement.

---

# 5. One Community / Ten Use-Case Experiment

Early Access should test one broad mission-aligned community through multiple practical use cases rather than prematurely declaring a single vertical.

Initial use-case matrix:

1. **Employment** — prepare for the interview, workplace conversation, or advancement opportunity.
2. **Entrepreneurship** — prepare, plan, communicate, and execute while building a company.
3. **Sales** — prepare for and execute conversations intended to move an opportunity forward.
4. **Negotiation** — prepare for difficult conversations and pursue a better outcome.
5. **Advocacy** — organize a position and communicate it effectively.
6. **Leadership** — navigate difficult or changing situations.
7. **Communication Support** — prepare what to say, understand the situation, and move forward.
8. **Creator Conversations** — engage in conversations that might otherwise be avoided because the creator is unfamiliar with the subject.
9. **Education / Learning** — understand enough to prepare and act more effectively.
10. **Personal Objectives** — give GEORGE an objective and use it to support progress toward that objective.

These are experiments and entry points, not separate products.

The Early Access program should measure which categories produce repeated, meaningful value.

# 6. What Early Access Funds

Public language should be simple:

> **Your Early Access purchase helps fund the testing, refinement, AI resources, infrastructure, and production work required to bring GEORGE forward.**

The campaign may describe the work in these categories:

- real-world testing;
- refinement and reliability;
- AI/provider resources;
- LIVE testing and infrastructure;
- production hardening;
- privacy and security work;
- use-case validation;
- creator tools;
- monitoring and operational readiness.

Do not reduce the campaign to "buy OpenAI credits." AI resources are one component of the production transition.

Do not promise that every dollar is spent on a single technical item unless the campaign has actually established that allocation.

# 8. Funding Is a Milestone System

Do not initially frame the campaign as requiring enough money to support 20,000 users.

Use demand-driven milestones.

### Initial milestone — $2,500

**Finish, test, refine, and demonstrate.**

### Next milestone — amount TBD from actual usage

**Expand Early Access.**

### Next milestone — amount TBD

**Expand Founding Creator distribution and use-case validation.**

### Scale milestone — amount TBD

**Increase infrastructure and intelligence capacity toward thousands of users.**

The 20,000-user model is a scale objective, not the initial funding requirement.

# 9. Early Access Purchase Structure

Current product tiers:

- **Smart — Free**
- **Intelligent — $9.99/month**
- **Brilliant — $25/month**

These prices remain subject to unit-economics validation.

The public campaign should explain the tiers through capability and access, not token counts or internal model mechanics.

### Smart

A free way to experience GEORGE and expand access to people who may benefit most but have limited resources.

### Intelligent — $9.99

Everyday GEORGE for planning, communication, execution, and access to more capable support when needed.

### Brilliant — $25

A higher-capability GEORGE experience for users who expect to rely on it more heavily and for more demanding objectives.

Brilliant may eventually include a meaningful ability to pass GEORGE to another person at a substantial discount. Exact mechanics and discount remain TBD.

# 10. Testing Is a Benefit, Not a Disclaimer

Early users should understand that testing is necessary to finish GEORGE well.

Public language:

> **Early Access isn't just about getting GEORGE early.**
>
> **You're helping us put GEORGE into real situations so we can discover where it creates the greatest value, where it needs to improve, and where it needs to become exceptional.**
>
> **Sales. Negotiations. Founding companies. Employment. Leadership. Communication. Accessibility. Creator conversations. And situations we haven't anticipated yet.**

Users should understand that real-world usage helps validate and refine the product while preserving privacy boundaries.

---

# 11. Privacy-Safe Validation Message

Public-facing principle:

> **GEORGE can learn where it becomes more effective across real-world use cases without turning private conversations into a shared data pool.**

Supporting language:

> **We want to measure what improves GEORGE—not who you are.**

Only minimum necessary metadata should be used for aggregate product improvement, subject to implementation and privacy review.

Do not claim that privacy-safe use-case telemetry is fully implemented unless verified against the current runtime.

# 12. Founding Creator Program

The first operational goal is not 100 active creators simultaneously.

The first goal is to identify and activate a small number of high-fit creators, learn what works, and then expand toward the 100-creator target.

Initial creator outreach target:

**10 creator contacts per day.**

Target creator categories:

- career / employment;
- founders / entrepreneurship;
- sales / telemarketing;
- negotiation / business;
- creators / interviewers;
- small business;
- community development;
- accessibility / disability outreach;
- education / skill development;
- advocacy / civic / political commentary where product fit exists;
- demographic/community creators;
- other trusted creators whose audiences have high-impact objectives.

Creator selection criterion:

> **Does this creator have an audience that is trying to accomplish something GEORGE could help them accomplish?**

Secondary criterion:

> **Does the creator personally understand why this capability could matter to their audience?**

## **Founding Creator**

**Founding Principal**

# 14. Creator Communication Principle

The creator is not being asked to sell an abstract AI concept.

The creator is being invited to show their audience a capability that may help them accomplish something important.

Core creator message:

> **Instead of simply asking AI a question, you give GEORGE an objective—and GEORGE helps you move toward it.**

Execution language should emphasize:

- prepare;
- communicate;
- execute;
- move the conversation forward;
- handle unexpected questions;
- pursue an objective;
- have difficult conversations;
- operate in unfamiliar situations;
- keep moving when circumstances change.

Avoid describing internal processes on the public-facing creator surface.

# 16. Direct Creator Outreach

First-contact communication should be short and personal.

Working structure:

1. Identify why the creator's audience is a fit.
2. Explain that GEORGE is already beyond concept/core development.
3. Explain that Early Access is being used for real-world testing and refinement.
4. Offer a Founding Creator position.
5. Explain that the creator gets a personal link, QR, and teleprompter.
6. Explain that qualifying revenue participation will be established after unit economics are complete.
7. Invite them to see GEORGE before committing.

Do not send a long technical explanation in the first message.

# 23. Public Disclosure Boundary

Early users and creators may be told that GEORGE contains sophisticated layers for:

- transcript processing;
- signal detection;
- reasoning stages;
- provider resources;
- model selection;
- memory;
- latency handling;
- delivery policies;
- orchestration.

Public-facing descriptions should explain **what these capabilities accomplish**, not their implementation.

Do not disclose:

- prompt architecture;
- routing logic;
- transcript pipeline implementation;
- model-selection rules;
- proprietary signal logic;
- memory retrieval mechanics;
- internal orchestration;
- algorithms;
- thresholds;
- proprietary decision structures;
- source code.

Public principle:

> **Show the capability. Protect the choreography.**

IP language should remain concise:

> **We're willing to show you what GEORGE can do. We don't need to give away the blueprint for how it does it.**

Patent and other IP protection strategy remains a company-level protection program and should not become a distracting part of the consumer fundraising page.

---

# 24. Commercial / Legal Guardrails

This Early Access apparatus is for product access, support, testing, validation, and creator distribution—not an offer of securities.

Do not use investment language such as:

- invest in GEORGE;
- ownership;
- equity;
- return on investment;
- investor benefits;
- guaranteed appreciation.

unless a separately reviewed securities offering is intentionally created and legally structured.

Creator revenue participation must have written commercial terms before payment obligations are activated.

No commission percentage is final until the unit-economics audit is complete.

---

# 25. Immediate Build Order

Do not begin with creator automation.

Build the smallest functioning fundraising loop first:

### A. Owned Early Access page

One canonical URL that explains GEORGE and accepts the primary CTA.

### B. Payment/access

Connect the selected payment mechanism to the Early Access tiers and/or support contribution path.

### C. Attribution

Generate creator-specific links and campaign/source identifiers.

### D. Creator package

Provide each accepted creator with:

- personal link;
- QR;
- teleprompter;
- short product explanation;
- approved claims;
- support contact/process.

### E. User onboarding

Capture the user's intended use-case category and introduce them to GEORGE without unnecessary friction.

### F. Success feedback

Capture minimum necessary product-improvement signals.

### G. Financial measurement

Reconcile payment revenue, AI/provider usage, infrastructure cost, refunds, and creator attribution.

Only after this loop works should we automate or expand aggressively.

---

# 26. Launch Gate

The first campaign should not launch publicly until these are verified:

- Early Access page works;
- payment/access flow works;
- user receives access correctly;
- creator attribution works;
- creator QR/link works;
- onboarding works;
- privacy language is accurate;
- Smart / Intelligent / Brilliant behavior matches the actual product;
- refund/support path exists;
- provider credentials and billing are verified;
- initial testing budget is funded;
- monitoring exists;
- known production limitations are documented;
- first test cohort can be supported.

The first public objective is **credible demonstration and validation**, not maximum traffic.
